Questions parents ask

Do I need an appointment for the emergency room?

No. The pediatric emergency and NICU are open 24/7 with no appointment. Walk in any time; a triage nurse sees every child within five minutes.

Can both parents stay overnight?

Yes. Every inpatient room has two attendant beds, and one parent can stay in the NICU family room overnight.

Which vaccines are walk-in and which need a slot?

All routine IAP vaccines are walk-in between 9:00 and 12:00 daily. Rabies, typhoid boosters for travel, and catch-up plans need a booked slot so a doctor can review the history.

Do you offer video consultations?

Yes, for follow-ups and reports review, Monday to Saturday. New symptoms are always seen in person.
